# Green Goddess Soup
## Ingredients
- 2 zucchinis, halved lengthwise
- 2 onions, quartered
- 3–4 yellow potatoes, cut into ½–¾ inch pieces
- ~3 cups broccoli florets
- 2 heads of garlic, tops cut off
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp each dried thyme, paprika & chilli flakes
- Salt & pepper to taste
- 1 cup frozen peas, boiled
- Handful of spinach
- 3 cups vegetable broth, plus more as needed

## Instructions
1. Add zucchini, onions, potatoes, broccoli and garlic to a parchment lined baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and season with thyme, paprika, chilli flakes, salt and pepper.
2. Roast at 400°F for 35–45 minutes, stirring halfway through, until tender and golden.
3. Add the roasted veggies (squeeze the garlic out of its skin) to a blender with the peas, spinach and 3 cups of vegetable broth. Blend until smooth, adding more broth to reach your desired consistency.
4. Top with coconut milk and pesto, and serve with a slice of sourdough.
