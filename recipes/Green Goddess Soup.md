# Green Goddess Soup
## Ingredients
- 2 zucchinis, halved lengthwise
- 2 onions, quartered
- 3–4 yellow potatoes, cut into 1–2cm pieces
- ~250g broccoli florets
- 2 heads of garlic, tops cut off
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp each dried thyme, paprika & chilli flakes
- Salt & pepper to taste
- ~140g frozen peas, boiled
- Handful of spinach
- 750ml vegetable broth, plus more as needed

## Instructions
1. Add zucchini, onions, potatoes, broccoli and garlic to a parchment lined baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and season with thyme, paprika, chilli flakes, salt and pepper.
2. Roast at 200°C (180°C fan) for 35–45 minutes, stirring halfway through, until tender and golden.
3. Add the roasted veggies (squeeze the garlic out of its skin) to a blender with the peas, spinach and 750ml of vegetable broth. Blend until smooth, adding more broth to reach your desired consistency.
4. Top with coconut milk and pesto, and serve with a slice of sourdough.
