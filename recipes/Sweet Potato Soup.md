# Sweet Potato Soup
## Ingredients
- 2 large sweet potatoes (peeled), either in halves as I did or smaller pieces so it roasts faster!
- 2 red bell peppers, remove the seeds before or after roasting
- 4 carrots (peeled)
- 2 red onions, quartered
- 1 or 2 heads of garlic, tops cut off
- 3-4 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp each: cinnamon, paprika, dried thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Vegetable broth (about 750ml–1 litre, or as needed)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
2. Place all prepared vegetables on a parchment-lined baking tray with the garlic heads cut-side up.
3. Drizzle generously with olive oil and season with cinnamon, paprika, thyme, salt and pepper.
4. Roast for 35–40 minutes, or until the sweet potatoes are fork-tender and the vegetables are nicely roasted (be careful not to burn!).
5. Transfer everything to a blender, squeezing the roasted garlic out of its skin. Add vegetable broth and blend until smooth, adding more broth until you reach your desired consistency.
6. Serve topped with coconut milk, fresh herbs and a drizzle of chilli oil if you'd like. Enjoy with sourdough bread!
