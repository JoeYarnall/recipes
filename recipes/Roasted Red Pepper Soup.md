# Roasted Red Pepper Soup
## Ingredients
- 3 red bell peppers (cut in half)
- ~1.8kg tomatoes (I used 2 large hot house, 2 Roma & a variety of cherry tomatoes - but use whatever combo you'd like!)
- 2 onions, halved
- 2 heads of garlic
- 2-4 tbsp olive or avocado oil
- ½ tsp each dried basil, dried oregano, dried thyme, chili flakes and paprika
- Salt & pepper, to taste
- Pinch of brown sugar
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 500–750ml vegetable broth, depending on desired consistency

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
2. Add bell peppers, tomatoes, onions and garlic to a large parchment-lined sheet pan. Drizzle with olive oil and season with all the spices.
3. Roast for 40–50 minutes, until the veggies are soft and nicely charred on top.
4. Transfer roasted vegetables to a blender. Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins.
5. Add brown sugar, balsamic vinegar and 500ml vegetable broth. Blend until smooth, adding more broth as needed to reach your desired consistency.
6. Heat it up in a pot or microwave and adjust seasonings as you see fit!
7. Serve topped with a drizzle of chili oil, coconut milk, fresh herbs and sourdough.
